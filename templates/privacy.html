Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

1. Data We Collect

Spendly collects the personal expense data you enter, including categories, amounts, dates, and descriptions, as well as basic account information such as your name and email address.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide expense tracking features such as summaries, category breakdowns, and spending insights. We do not use your financial data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in accordance with industry standards. Access is limited to you and authorized personnel who maintain the service.

4. Third Party Services

We may use third party services for hosting, analytics, and email delivery. These providers are bound to process data only as needed to operate the service.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please reach out to us through the contact information provided in the app.
